Background

Yappity Yap is the first game jam project I worked on, alongside my lovely friend, Crim. It's a small game where you take on the role of a VTuber, and have to answer crazy chat messages and try not to get cancelled!

How to Play

  1. 1Read the highlighted chat message.
  2. 2Pick one of three responses.
  3. 3Correct answers slow chat down, and incorrect ones speed it up.
  4. 4Survive as long as you can without getting "cancelled!"
